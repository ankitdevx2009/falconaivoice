Policies

Plain-language service policies. Last updated: November 21, 2026. These are general website terms, not legal advice; applicable consumer and privacy rights still apply.

Privacy policy

Information we handle. Firebase Authentication manages sign-in. Falcon Voice receives your account email and user ID and stores account balances, generation counts, and payment/order records to operate the service.

Text and audio. Text, selected voice and language are sent to Cartesia to generate speech. The app does not intentionally save your prompt text or generated WAV on its server; generated audio is returned to your browser for playback/download. Avoid submitting confidential or sensitive personal information in scripts.

Payments and providers. Razorpay processes payments; Falcon Voice does not store payment card details. Firebase, Cartesia and Razorpay process information under their own privacy terms. Browser storage may be used by Firebase to keep you signed in.

Retention and requests. Account, credit and transaction records are retained as needed to provide the service, maintain balances and address payment or legal issues. To ask about, correct or request deletion of your account data, contact officialsmpankit@gmail.com. Deleting an account may affect remaining credits or access where permitted by law.

Sharing and security. We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with the providers above to deliver requested features, or when required to protect the service or comply with law. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Terms of service

Falcon Voice provides AI text-to-speech generation, available voice/language filters, browser playback and WAV downloads. Features and voices may change or be temporarily unavailable.

  • One credit is used for one successful audio generation. If generation fails, the credit is automatically returned.
  • Credit packs are one-time purchases; credits do not expire. Current prices and pack sizes are shown on the home page before purchase.
  • You are responsible for the text you submit and must have the rights and permissions needed to use it. Do not use the service for unlawful, deceptive, abusive or non-consensual impersonation.
  • Use generated audio in line with applicable law and the terms of the voice and generation providers. Output quality and availability are not guaranteed.
  • Keep your account credentials private and notify us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Falcon Voice is operated by Ankit Choudhary. These terms are subject to applicable law and do not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

Payments, cancellations and refunds

Credit packs are one-time digital purchases and do not renew automatically. A credit is charged only for a successful generation; failed generations are credited back automatically.

If payment was deducted but credits were not added, or you see a duplicate charge, contact support by email with the Razorpay payment ID. Refund requests are reviewed under applicable law and payment-provider rules. Nothing in this policy removes mandatory consumer rights.
