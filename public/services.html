What you can do

Falcon Voice services

Create speech audio from text, choose from the available voice catalog, and download the result for your projects.

Text to speech

Turn a script into spoken audio with the available AI voices. Each successful generation uses one credit; failed generations return the credit automatically.

Language and voice filters

Browse available languages and filter voices by the provider’s listed voice type. Hindi, English and other catalog languages may be available; options depend on the provider catalog.

Listen and download

Preview generated audio in the browser and download it as a WAV file. Audio generation depends on service availability and the selected voice.

Credit packs

Choose a one-time credit pack on the home page. Credits do not expire. Payments are processed through Razorpay.
